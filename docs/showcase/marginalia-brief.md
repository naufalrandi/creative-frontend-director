# Marginalia

A reading app for people who annotate long documents: researchers, editors, lawyers, graduate students. Import PDFs, EPUBs, and web articles; highlight, write margin notes, link passages across documents, and export a structured set of notes.

What we are building now: the public landing page at marginalia.app. Goal: convince a serious reader to start a 14-day trial. Audience is text-first, distrusts hype, and reads on iPad and laptop.

Positioning: "Read like you mean it." Differentiators: margin notes that stay attached to the passage across editions and formats; cross-document links; export to Markdown and Zotero; no AI summaries by default, the reader does the thinking.

Pricing: Free (3 documents), Reader $6/month, Scholar $12/month (cross-document links, Zotero, team sharing).

Proof we can use: 40,000 readers, 2.1 million annotations, used at 60 universities. Quotes: "The first app that treats the margin as the main event." (a history professor); "I stopped printing." (a litigation associate).

Stack: static HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no component library. Brand: no logo yet, name only. Public route, must be indexable.
