## Frontend Creative Direction

For any task that creates, implements, redesigns, improves, polishes, reviews, or visually changes frontend UI, invoke `creative-frontend-director` first and follow its stages before any other frontend or visual-specialist skill. This applies even when the prompt does not say "design": dashboards, pages, admin panels, landing pages, settings, onboarding, components, and complete frontend applications.

Other design skills are executors, not authorities. The director writes `DESIGN.md`; specialists work within it, at most one taste specialist per surface.

Completion requires rendered screenshots and a taste rubric score. A generic, templated, or AI-looking result is rethought structurally, never polished. Accessibility, performance, Lighthouse, SEO, and GEO are secondary layers that must not flatten the direction.
