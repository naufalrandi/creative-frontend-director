# Product Signature

A product signature is one recognizable design behavior grounded in product meaning. Every substantial project defines one in `DESIGN.md` section 10.

## Good Sources

- a dominant workflow made visible in the layout
- the information structure users think in
- a domain metaphor translated abstractly, never literally
- data behavior such as change over time, thresholds, or ordering
- a repeated user action given a distinctive, consistent treatment

## Examples

- Monitoring: a continuous signal and event language that connects every change over time.
- Finance: an ordered ledger rhythm where exceptions visibly interrupt certainty.
- Creative editor: the workspace composition gradually mirrors the final published output.
- Support desk: every conversation surface keeps the customer's timeline pinned as the spine of the page.

## Not A Signature

- a random gradient or accent
- the logo shape repeated everywhere
- gimmicky domain illustration
- unusual controls for novelty
- a visual metaphor taken literally

## Test

The signature must improve coherence or comprehension. If removing it makes the product easier to use, it is decoration, not a signature.
